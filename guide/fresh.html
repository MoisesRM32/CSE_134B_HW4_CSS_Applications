Fresh Cheeses

Fresh cheeses are young cheeses that are eaten soon after they are made. They are usually soft, moist, and mild because they have not spent time aging.

Common examples include mozzarella, ricotta, mascarpone, cottage cheese, and fresh goat cheese. Their flavor is often milky, creamy, and slightly tangy.

Because fresh cheeses contain more moisture than aged cheeses, they should be refrigerated and eaten within a short period of time.
